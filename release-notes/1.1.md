- Three tabs everywhere: Schedule, Classes and Settings, the same on the Mac, iPhone and iPad.
- Schedule shows your next two weeks with each class's status; skip, cancel or stop a weekly class right there. Away dates live here too.
- Add a class (every week or just once) from the phone as well as the Mac, with how fast each class fills.
- The Mac app opens like any app, shows earlier classes this week, and now updates itself.
- Signing in happens right in the window, with a welcome screen on first launch.
