- Booking alerts read simply "Booked by your Mac".
- MacBooks show a laptop icon, and the booking Mac appears by name ("Mac mini is booking 11 classes").
- The menu bar pop-up has one "Open YMCA Booker" button instead of three that all opened the same window.
- Opens on early macOS and iOS 27.2 betas, which crashed at launch with build .2.
